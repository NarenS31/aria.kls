# Figures and tables for the CALE-AI paper

All figures: vector PDF, 3.3 in wide (AAAI single column), all text 9 pt at final size, TrueType fonts embedded (pdf/ps fonttype 42; `pdffonts` shows no Type 3), no titles inside the plot, PDF metadata cleared. They use the shared style in `scripts/cale/figstyle.py`: setting colour Quiet `#2457A6` with circle markers, and Okabe-Ito colours for everything else (vermillion `#D55E00` for wrong answers, orange `#E69F00` triangles for logged ASSISTments hints, gray for the threshold). No test data are used anywhere.

## Figure 1: the entanglement timeline (`figures/fig1_entanglement.pdf`)

**Script:** `scripts/cale/fig1_entanglement.py`. The script records its selection in `figures/fig1_selection.json`. Run it from `scripts/cale/` with the project Python.

**Data:** 2009-10 **validation** students only. Test students are dropped right after the split, and the script asserts that none reach the table.

- The risk is the frozen pipeline's BKT stuck risk: BKT fitted on train (`min_seq` from `frozen_val.json`) with the frozen Platt calibrator. This is the same quantity the validation evaluation and CALE E2/E5 use.
- Logged ASSISTments hints are `hint_count > 0`, the CALE E5 indicator, aligned with the E5 helper.
- No number from this figure enters the text or `numbers.json`.

**Selection rule (fixed in the script before any candidate was looked at).** The figure shows the first sequence, in the order of `policy.Table(validation, include_leavers=False)`, that meets all five conditions:
1. it is stuck (y = 1);
2. it has at least 3 verified errors at decision points (tries 3-9);
3. its calibrated risk is below the Quiet threshold 0.57 at its first decision point and reaches at least 0.57 at a later one;
4. the Quiet policy fires (a verified error with risk ≥ 0.57) at some try i\*;
5. ASSISTments logged a hint at some try in 3..i\*, so in the same window as, and no later than, ARIA's first hint.

The table order is the loader's order: students by `user_id` and, within a student, assignments by first `order_id`. It is deterministic.

**Picked:** position 10 in that order (positions 0-9 failed at least one condition).
- Skill: *Divisibility Rules*. 10 opportunities; stuck.
- Sequence hash `753b61157996`, which is `sha256("user_id:assignment_id")[:12]`. The raw ids are not stored in the paper materials.
- Answers at tries 1-10: `1 1 0 0 1 0 1 0 1 1` (all first actions are answer attempts).
- ASSISTments logged hints at **tries 3 and 8**: 2 hints each.
- Verified errors at decision tries 3, 4, 6 and 8. Calibrated risk at tries 3-9: 0.16, 0.27, 0.18, 0.43, 0.37, 1.00, 1.00.
- The Quiet policy's first hint would come at **try 8**, the same try at which ASSISTments had already given two hints. ASSISTments had also hinted at try 3, five tries before ARIA would act.

**Why it shows the problem.** In the replay, the outcome that labels this sequence "stuck" happened after ASSISTments had already helped twice inside the decision window. Replay cannot separate what ARIA's hint would have added from help the student already received (CALE E5, E7).

**Caveat for the caption.** Risk is exactly 1.00 at tries 8 and 9 for a structural reason, not because the model is confident. After a wrong answer at try 8, three correct answers in a row by try 10 are no longer possible, so the stuck label is already determined. The caption should say this, or a reader may read the jump as model confidence.

## Trade-off curve (`figures/fig2_tradeoff.pdf`; the paper's Figure 1)

**Script:** `scripts/cale/fig2_tradeoff.py` (plot only; reads `aria_pace/results_cale/e1_tradeoff.json`, recomputes nothing). Pooled CV held-out folds, post hoc (CALE E1). BKT, PFA and logistic-risk threshold sweeps with 95% student-bootstrap bands; the k-in-a-row rule (k = 1-5) and matched-random baselines as points with 95% student-bootstrap error bars; the three frozen BKT settings as distinct markers (Quiet circle, Balanced square, Supportive triangle). The older `aria_pace/results_cale/figures/e1_tradeoff.pdf` is left as is.

Note on numbering: `fig1_entanglement.pdf` is the paper's Figure 2 and `fig2_tradeoff.pdf` is the paper's Figure 1; the file names were kept as requested.
