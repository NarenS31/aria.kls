## What is this?

We are studying a learning tool called ARIA. It reads what you type or say about
a school problem and asks a question intended to help you think through it.
ARIA is automated. It can misunderstand you and can be wrong.

## What would I do?

If you choose to participate, you will [approved number and length of sessions],
work on [topic] problems, and complete short learning checks. We will record
[approved data]. A trained adult will be available.

## Do I have to?

No. Joining is your choice. You can skip a question, ask for a person, or stop
at any time. Your choice will not change your grade, classes, services, or how
teachers treat you. Even if your parent says yes, you may say no.

## What might be uncomfortable?

Some problems may feel difficult or frustrating. ARIA may misunderstand what
you mean. Tell the supervising adult or stop if you are uncomfortable.
