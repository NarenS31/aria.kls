# Outcome measurement plan

## Principle

ARIA exposure logs and classifier outputs are process measures. They cannot be
the sole evidence that ARIA improved learning. Primary outcomes are collected
after tutoring on independently authored tasks.

## Content-learning instrument

- Define one initial construct, such as solving and justifying multi-step linear
  equations.
- Create parallel pretest, immediate post-test, and delayed-test forms from a
  shared blueprint without reusing practice items.
- Balance forms on content, representation, difficulty, and scoring demand.
- Include constructed responses so reasoning can be scored, not only answers.
- Two independent educators review construct coverage, correctness, alternate
  solutions, reading load, and form comparability.
- Pilot item difficulty and revise before the confirmatory study.
- Outcome scorers are trained and blinded to condition.

## Primary score

The prespecified total content score combines item points using the locked
rubric. Report reliability appropriate to the item format and the score
distribution. Do not change weights after inspecting treatment differences.

## Unprompted transfer

After tutoring, students solve novel tasks with ARIA absent. Human coders use
the frozen observable-move taxonomy to code:

- task orientation and planning;
- strategy steps and justification;
- monitoring/checking;
- evaluation and self-correction;
- help-seeking.

Correctness and strategy use are separate outcomes. More metacognitive language
is not automatically better if the reasoning is wrong or inefficient.

ARIA's in-product prospective transfer log uses an equally strict event rule.
All conditions must hold: the previous turn contained no ARIA prompt; the task
ID changed; the student produced a PLAN or MONITORING move; the move referenced
the active task; and a later turn showed execution of that plan. Prompted
planning is stored as a separate process measure. A detected plan that is never
executed is not a transfer event.

## Calibration

Before each outcome task, collect a 1–5 confidence judgment. Primary
calibration summaries:

- absolute confidence–accuracy error;
- overconfidence and underconfidence separately;
- resolution, the association between confidence and correctness across items.

These are secondary outcomes and require enough items per participant.

## Retention

Administer the delayed form 7–14 days later under the same assistance rules.
Report completion and differential attrition. A later test is not “retention”
if students can continue practicing with one condition in between unless that
exposure is measured and incorporated into the design.

## Measures not sufficient alone

- self-reported helpfulness;
- time spent;
- number of messages;
- classifier state transitions;
- answer accuracy on practice items exposed during tutoring;
- LLM-judged response quality;
- one developer's longitudinal trace.

These may explain implementation but cannot replace independent learning
outcomes.
