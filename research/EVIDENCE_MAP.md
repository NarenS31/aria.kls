# Evidence-to-design map

Last reviewed: 2026-07-29.

This is a design rationale, not proof that ARIA works. Each row identifies the
source, the limited proposition it supports, the ARIA decision it motivates,
and what must still be tested.

| Evidence area | Primary or authoritative source | Supported proposition | ARIA design implication | What the source does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Observable SRL processes | Fan et al. (2021), [Temporal Assessment of Self-Regulated Learning by Mining Students' Think-Aloud Protocols](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.749749) | Think-alouds can be human-coded for task analysis, monitoring, evaluation, elaboration, and related observable activities. | Research labels use observable moves with exact evidence spans. | That ARIA's automatic labels are accurate. |
| Validity of SRL traces | Fan et al. (2022), [Towards investigating the validity of measurement of self-regulated learning based on trace data](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09291-1) | SRL measurement requires theory-to-observation mapping and validation; trace interpretations are not self-validating. | Behavioral logs and language labels remain separate measurements and require human validation. | That clicks, pauses, or text alone reveal a hidden state. |
| Context in discourse classification | Suresh et al. (2022), [Fine-tuning Transformers with Additional Context to Classify Discursive Moves](https://aclanthology.org/2022.bea-1.11/) | Adjacent dialogue context improved classification of real classroom discourse moves. | ARIA evaluates contextual utterance windows and splits tests by student/session. | That ARIA's current classifier generalizes to real students. |
| Real K-12 discourse data | Suresh et al. (2022), [TalkMoves Dataset](https://aclanthology.org/2022.lrec-1.497/) | Human-transcribed K-12 mathematics discourse can be annotated for observable teacher and student moves. | External validation should include real, human-annotated classroom language. | That TalkMoves directly represents one-to-one ARIA use or English tasks. |
| Pedagogical tutor evaluation | Kochmar et al. (2025), [BEA Shared Task on Pedagogical Ability Assessment](https://aclanthology.org/2025.bea-1.77/) | Human gold labels distinguish mistake identification, mistake location, guidance, and actionability; current automatic systems remain imperfect. | Blinded educator ratings use those dimensions plus answer leakage and learner ownership. | That an LLM judge is equivalent to an educator. |
| Grounded tutoring dialogue | Macina et al. (2023), [MathDial](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.findings-emnlp.372) | Tutor models may provide incorrect feedback or reveal solutions; teacher-authored scaffolding moves and problem grounding matter. | Every research task needs an answer key, solution paths, misconception evidence, and graded hints. | That MathDial-trained behavior improves ARIA learning outcomes. |
| Curriculum-embedded metacognition | Education Endowment Foundation (2025), [Metacognition and Self-Regulated Learning guidance](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/metacognition) | Planning, monitoring, and evaluation should be explicitly taught, modeled, scaffolded, and embedded in subject instruction. | ARIA combines task-specific strategy support with metacognitive prompts and gradually removes scaffolds. | That a single generic question causes durable transfer. |
| Transfer of metacognitive skill | Schuster et al. (2020), [experimental training study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09237-5) | Hybrid training combining metacognitive and cognitive strategies improved near and far transfer in the studied context, while observed skill use remained limited. | ARIA's transfer outcome must combine domain strategy use with unprompted regulation on new tasks. | That ARIA produces the same effect or that far transfer is automatic. |
| Mixed prompt effects | Engelmann et al. (2021), [self-created metacognitive prompts](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41039-021-00148-w) | One experimental study found no clear overall learning effect, while prompt utilization related to later performance. | Log whether a prompt is used and retain null outcomes; do not equate prompt delivery with learning. | That prompts are universally effective. |
| Rigorous causal designs | U.S. What Works Clearinghouse (2026), [Procedures and Standards Handbook v4](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Docs/referenceresources/wwc_standards_handbook_v4.pdf) | Randomization, attrition, baseline equivalence, eligible outcomes, and absence of confounds determine causal credibility. | The student study uses an active control, tracks attrition, measures baseline performance, and reports assignment integrity. | That a small uncontrolled pilot supports causal claims. |
| Statistical power in education | Hedges & Rhoads (2010), [Statistical Power Analysis in Education Research](https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/resource-library/report/research-report/statistical-power-analysis-education-research) | Clustered education designs require power calculations that account for intraclass correlation and design structure. | Power scenarios include clustering, covariate adjustment, and attrition. | The exact sample required without empirical variance and ICC estimates. |
| Research with minors | U.S. HHS OHRP, [Research with Children FAQs](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/guidance/faq/children-research/index.html) | Research with children commonly requires IRB determination, parental permission, and affirmative assent. | No student recruitment begins until institutional review and approved permission/assent procedures. | That these repository templates are approved forms. |
| Student record privacy | U.S. Department of Education, [FERPA studies guidance](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/faq/may-educational-agency-or-institution-disclose-personally-identifiable-information-students) | Studies using education-record PII on behalf of a school require a qualifying purpose and written agreement under the studies exception. | Prefer study-created IDs; execute a data agreement before receiving school records; define destruction dates. | That all ARIA interaction logs are automatically FERPA education records in every setting. |
| ADHD school support | U.S. CDC (2026), [ADHD in the Classroom](https://www.cdc.gov/adhd/treatment/classroom.html) | Evidence is stronger for behavioral classroom management and organizational training than for many individual accommodations; clear assignments, feedback, breaks, and individualized planning are commonly recommended with important evidence limitations. | Offer adjustable brevity, clear next actions, breaks, and student choice as accessibility options for everyone; study them rather than branding the interface as ADHD-effective. | That ARIA treats ADHD or that its interface improves outcomes for students with ADHD. |

## Design conclusion

The literature supports testing a task-grounded, fading metacognitive scaffold.
It also shows why ARIA must not assume that delivering a question equals
learning, that a language model can infer internal states, or that synthetic
performance transfers to real students. The research program therefore
separates response quality, language measurement, feasibility, learning,
retention, and unprompted transfer.
