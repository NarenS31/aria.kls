Dear Dr. Borchers and Ms. Zhang,

I am writing to request access to the think-aloud dataset accompanying your EDM
2024 paper, *"Using Large Language Models to Detect Self-Regulated Learning in
Think-Aloud Protocols"* (Zhang, Borchers, Aleven, & Baker).

**Who we are.** We are developing ARIA, a research prototype that detects a
student's cognitive state from their think-aloud text and responds with a single
metacognitive prompt (never the answer). ARIA is currently evaluated only on a
synthetic think-aloud corpus generated by open LLMs, which is a circular setup.
Your dataset is the only resource we are aware of that pairs **real** student
think-aloud text with expert self-regulated-learning (SRL) labels, so it is
uniquely suited to breaking that circularity.

**Intended use.** Non-commercial academic research only. Specifically, we would:

1. Run ARIA's cognitive-state classifier on your think-aloud segments and report
   agreement with your SRL labels via a documented SRL→ARIA crosswalk (below).
2. Report accuracy, macro-F1, and a confusion matrix against your labels as a
   *direct* (real-label) external validation, alongside an honest error analysis.
3. Store the data only on access-controlled machines, never redistribute it, and
   delete it on request or at project end.

We will cite the dataset and paper (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.12729790) in any
resulting write-up, and we are happy to share our results and code with you
before publication.

**ARIA's taxonomy.** ARIA uses seven cognitive states:
PLANNING, FLOW, CONFUSED, RUSHING, FRUSTRATED, STUCK, INSIGHT.

**Proposed SRL → ARIA crosswalk** (we would value your feedback on this;
ambiguous SRL codes are mapped to *none* rather than forced):

| SRL code (think-aloud) | ARIA state | Confidence | Rationale |
|------------------------|------------|------------|-----------|
| planning / orientation / goal-setting | PLANNING | high/med | forethought-phase strategy setting |
| prior-knowledge activation | PLANNING | medium | forethought-phase preparation |
| elaboration / organization / strategy use | FLOW | med/low | productive on-task processing |
| confusion / struggle | CONFUSED | high/med | detected difficulty |
| help-seeking | STUCK | medium | reached an impasse |
| negative affect / frustration | FRUSTRATED | high | emotional dysregulation |
| insight / positive re-evaluation | INSIGHT | high/low | realization moment |
| monitoring (bare) | — (none) | — | state-ambiguous without valence |
| evaluation / reflection (bare) | — (none) | — | spans multiple ARIA states |
| off-task / motivation | — (none) | — | no ARIA cognitive-state equivalent |

An adapter implementing exactly this crosswalk is already written against the
published schema, so we can begin the analysis immediately upon access.

Could you let us know the process and any data-use agreement we should sign? We
are glad to accommodate any constraints you require.

Thank you very much for making this work possible, and for considering our
request.
