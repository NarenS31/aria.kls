# ARIA-Pace v3: what the additional analyses found

Branch `aria-pace-v3`, from `origin/aria-pace`. **Nothing here changes a published number, a frozen file, or the demo.** No locked split (2009 test, 2012 test) was read for anything new; the 2009 test students are dropped right after the split in every script, the 2012 file is not loaded. Every analysis was written as a dated amendment in `aria_pace/SPEC.md` (A4, A5, A6) in a commit **before** its code and results existed. Frozen files are byte-identical to `origin/aria-pace` (sha256 prefixes: `frozen_val.json` 057f4389adbc, `frozen_v2.json` c455ed708157).

| 1. Mastery-definition sensitivity | A4; **exploratory sensitivity** (validation only); control check pre-registered | Does the policy depend on how 'stuck' is defined? | Control D3 reproduces `frozen_val.json` exactly (Platt to 1e-9, taus 0.57 / 0.19 / 0.09). ## Negative or null results, stated plainly

- The headline saving is **not robust to the definition of mastery** on validation (Step 1): it is 50% under the frozen definition and 9% under two of the four alternatives. The published 52% is a result about the pre-registered definition, not about 'stuck' in general.
- **Multi-skill sequence models do not improve the decision** (Step 2), as the single-skill ones did not (`dkt_val.json`, `sakt_val.json`).
- **Detector-predicted affect adds nothing at the pre-registered margin** (Step 3, `test_results_2012.json`).
- **Replay cannot settle whether the policy is better than always helping**: that depends on the cost of leaving a student stuck, which no log measures (Step 4).

## What could not be done

- No new test number under any alternative definition or model: the 2009 and 2012 test splits are locked and spent (`TEST_LOCK`, `TEST_LOCK_2012`), and A5.5 requires a fresh, pre-declared holdout before any test number could exist.
- PFA and the logistic risk head were not re-run under the alternative mastery definitions.
- No data exist to estimate the effect of a prompt versus a hint, so Step 6 is a design note only.
- Safari or live-demo behaviour was not part of these steps.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, with `aria_pace/.venv` and the raw data in `aria_pace/data/raw/`: `python -m aria_pace.run_mastery_sens` then `python -m aria_pace.make_mastery_sens_report`; `python -m aria_pace.run_multiskill` then `python -m aria_pace.make_multiskill_report`; `python -m aria_pace.run_closed_loop` then `python -m aria_pace.make_closed_loop_report`; `python -m pytest aria_pace/tests`. The stress test needs a checkout of `summit-pace-mac`: `/usr/bin/python3 scripts/synthetic_calculus_stress.py --repo <checkout>`.
Seeds: split `20261015`; bootstrap `20261016` / `20261018` / `20261019`; paired AUC `20261024`; training `20261023`; simulation `20261101`; stress test `20261102`.
